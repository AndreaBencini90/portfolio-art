Toroidal Lattice

The Toroidal Lattice represents a space that folds back onto itself, abolishing boundaries. There is no exterior and no origin: every direction returns, as if the universe recognized only its own rhythm.

In Loop Quantum Gravity, spacetime is discrete and relational, often represented as a network of nodes, and gravity emerges from the relations between them.
