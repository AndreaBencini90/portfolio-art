← The Eight Molecules

Why these eight molecules

The project starts from a simple, radical idea: emotions are physical. Not metaphorically — literally, in the sense that they have a precise molecular structure we can measure and translate. The viewer doesn't see the molecule: they see its structural projection in a space of relations.

The biologist Helen Fisher describes romantic love as three distinct neurochemical systems — Desire, Attraction, Bonding. These eight molecules are their protagonists. They are all peptides: only peptides have a structure that folds, and can therefore become a graph. How the graph is born from a molecule is in the Method.

Phase 1 · the generalised drive

Desire

{% graphPlaceholder "#FF4D6B", "GnRH", "GnRH" %}

GnRH

The switch. The first signal in the hormonal cascade that activates desire: without this peptide, the system doesn't start.

{% graphPlaceholder "#B81B35", "Sostanza P", "Sostanza P" %}

Substance P

The neuropeptide of physical pain and separation pain — the same molecule. Desire and loss run on the same channel.

Phase 2 · focused obsession

Attraction

{% graphPlaceholder "#FFA93E", "Met-Enk PC", "Met-Encefalina PC" %}

Met-Enkephalin (PC)

The pleasure of physical contact. An endogenous opioid, measured in a neutral membrane.

{% graphPlaceholder "#F28E13", "Met-Enk Bic", "Met-Encefalina Bic" %}

Met-Enkephalin (Bic)

The same molecule in a different context: the conformation shifts with the environment. Emotions depend on context, not chemistry alone.

{% graphPlaceholder "#B86C0E", "CRF", "CRF" %}

CRF

Butterflies in the stomach: the pleasant anxiety of excitement. The line between stress and excitement is neurochemically thin.

Phase 3 · attachment

Bonding

{% graphPlaceholder "#4B92FF", "Ossitocina", "Ossitocina" %}

Oxytocin

The hormone of recognition: it turns a stranger into someone of yours. Trust has a molecular structure.

{% graphPlaceholder "#2274F2", "Vasopressina", "Vasopressina" %}

Vasopressin

Nearly identical to oxytocin, opposite effects: territoriality, jealousy, control. The shadow side of bonding.

{% graphPlaceholder "#1A58B8", "Beta-Endorfina", "Beta-Endorfina" %}

β-Endorphin

Euphoria. An endogenous opioid 30 times more potent than morphine: the feeling that the world has changed.

The idea: map a molecule onto its implicit statistical structure, then show the map without showing the molecule. If the work succeeds, the graph carries a formal quality coherent with the molecule's name — not because it was drawn that way, but because the statistical structure produces it. The discrepancy between these two cases is the wager.
